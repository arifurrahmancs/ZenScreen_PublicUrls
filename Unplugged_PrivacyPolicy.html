Privacy Policy

Unplugged: App Blocker & Focus Timer

This privacy policy applies to the Unplugged app (hereby referred to as "Application") for iPhone and iPad, created by Arifur Rahman (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as a Freemium service. The Application helps you block distracting apps and websites, set app time limits, run focus timers and view your screen time. It is provided "AS IS".

In short: your screen time data, blocked apps, schedules, photos and videos stay on your device. The Service Provider does not sell your data, does not show ads and does not require an account.

Screen Time Data

The Application uses Apple's Screen Time frameworks (Family Controls, Device Activity and Managed Settings) to block apps and websites and to show your screen time, pickups and activity reports. With these frameworks:

  • The apps, categories and websites you choose to block are given to the Application only as anonymous tokens created by Apple. The Service Provider cannot see which apps or websites you selected.
  • Your screen time and usage reports are calculated by iOS and shown inside a protected Apple extension on your device. This data is never sent to the Service Provider or to any third party.

Information Stored on Your Device

The following information is stored only on your device and is not uploaded by the Application:

  • Your Quick Timer, App Time Limit, Schedule Block and App Lock settings, focus sessions, streaks and app preferences.
  • Face Your Self: the short video you record or choose is saved inside the Application's private storage and is played back before you unlock an app. It is never uploaded. You can replace it in the Application, and it is removed when you delete the Application.
  • Touch Grass: the photo you take is checked on your device by a built-in machine learning model to confirm it shows grass. The photo is not saved or uploaded.
  • Your purchase status, so that premium features keep working offline.

Camera and Microphone

The Application asks for camera access only for the Touch Grass and Face Your Self interruptions, and microphone access only to record sound with your Face Your Self video. You can turn off either permission at any time in your device's Settings; these features will then not work.

Analytics and Crash Reports

To understand how the Application is used and to fix bugs, the Application uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics. These services may collect:

  • Which screens and features of the Application are used, and app events such as starting a focus session or opening the paywall.
  • Device model, operating system version, app version, language and an app instance identifier.
  • Approximate region (country or city level) derived from your IP address.
  • Crash logs and diagnostic information when the Application crashes.

This information is not linked to your name or email and does not include the apps or websites you block or your screen time data. The Application does not collect your precise location and does not use advertising identifiers for tracking. Links to the privacy policies of these services:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase
  • Firebase Crashlytics

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Apple through the App Store. The Service Provider does not receive your name, Apple ID, or payment details; the Application only receives confirmation from Apple of what you have purchased. Apple's handling of your data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Contacting Support

If you send feedback or a support request by email, the Service Provider receives your email address and anything you include in the message. This is used only to reply to you and is not shared.

How Information Is Used and Shared

Information is used only to run, maintain and improve the Application and to respond to you. The Service Provider does not sell, rent or share your personal information, except with the service providers listed above or when required by law.

Data Retention and Deletion

Data stored on your device stays until you delete it in the Application or delete the Application. Analytics and crash data are kept by Firebase according to its retention settings. To ask for deletion of any data held about you, contact arifurrahman.bc@gmail.com.

Children

The Application is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact the Service Provider and it will be deleted.

Security

The Service Provider uses reasonable measures to protect your information, including keeping most data on your device and storing purchase status in the iOS Keychain. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.
